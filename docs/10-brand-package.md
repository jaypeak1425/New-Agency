## 3. Visual Direction — 3 Moodboards

### Moodboard A: Premium Elegance (Recommended)
- **Color palette:** Deep navy (#1a2332), warm gold (#c9a961), soft cream (#f5f1e8), charcoal text (#2c2c2c)
- **Typography:** Serif headlines (Playfair Display or similar), clean sans-serif body (Inter or similar)
- **Imagery:** Premium photography — agents in tailored suits, conference rooms with warm lighting, mahogany desks, hand-signed documents
- **Feel:** Established, trust-coded, premium. Like a private bank. Like the agent is holding something rare.
- **References:** Apple Card visual, Goldman Sachs brand, Rolls-Royce website

### Moodboard B: Modern Tech-Forward
- **Color palette:** Dark mode primary (#0a0a0a), electric blue accent (#0066ff), white text
- **Typography:** Geometric sans-serif (Inter, SF Pro, or similar)
- **Imagery:** Clean UI screenshots, abstract data visualizations, modern office spaces
- **Feel:** Tech-forward, modern, clean. Like Linear, Vercel, or Notion.
- **References:** Linear app, Vercel website, modern fintech apps

### Moodboard C: Warm Authority
- **Color palette:** Forest green (#1a3a2e), warm cream (#f4f0e6), soft gold accent (#b8956a)
- **Typography:** Confident serif (Source Serif or similar), warm sans-serif (Lato or similar)
- **Imagery:** Real producers, real client meetings, warm interiors, genuine handshakes
- **Feel:** Trust + warmth + authority. Like a 100-year-old advisory firm that just got a tech upgrade.
- **References:** Edward Jones brand, modern Merrill Lynch, modern Vanguard

**Recommended pick:** Moodboard A (Premium Elegance) — best fit for "agent holding something expensive and rare"

## 4. Brand Voice & Tone Guide

The brand voice is Jay's Adventurer voice, applied across all three names.

### Voice attributes
- **Honest** — never overpromises, never uses "guarantee"
- **Plain-spoken** — talks to one person, not a crowd
- **Calm** — confident, not hyped
- **Conversational** — sounds like a friend, not a vendor
- **Practical** — focuses on what the agent can actually do, not abstract benefits

### Tone variations by context
- **Discovery call / sales page:** Direct, warm, clear. "Here's the engine. Here's what it does. Here's what it costs."
- **Email sequence:** Story-led, conversational. "So I've been thinking about something..." "Quick one for you today..."
- **Product UI / in-app:** Concise, helpful, never condescending. "Based on what you've told me, here are the strategies that fit."
- **IMO pitch deck:** Professional, B2B, data-backed. "Your agents can't get this anywhere else."

### Words to use
- Strategy, opportunity, engine, brain, case design, in your pocket, find, discover
- "I've got a guy" (the agent's own language)
- "The case design brain," "the strategy engine"

### Words to avoid
- "Guarantee" (in any form)
- "Tax-free" (use "non-taxable")
- "Money-back" / "premium-back"
- "Unlock your potential"
- "Transform your business"
- "Get rich"
- Specific income claims
- Guru language: "crush it," "kill it," "dominate"
- False urgency: "limited time," "act now," "don't miss out"

### Compliance language rules (baked into every asset)
- "Non-taxable" NEVER "tax-free"
- Always condition: "while the policy remains in force"
- No specific outcome quantification
- No IRS form numbers in client-facing copy
- Frame as the approach, not the product
- "Firm Advantage" — NEVER
- "CPA on staff" implication — NEVER

## 5. Brand Rules Checklist (Use This on Every Asset)

Before any asset goes public, check it against this list:

- [ ] No guarantee language
- [ ] No specific income claims
- [ ] No "tax-free" (use "non-taxable")
- [ ] No "Firm Advantage" or "CPA on staff" implication
- [ ] No "money-back guarantee" or "premium-back guarantee"
- [ ] All benefit claims conditioned with "while the policy remains in force"
- [ ] No IRS form numbers in client-facing copy
- [ ] The promise is based on the agent's own book, not invented numbers
- [ ] The voice is honest, plain-spoken, calm — not hyped
- [ ] The visual is premium, elegant, defensible
- [ ] The CTA is one clear action, not three options
- [ ] Disclosure is prominent (paid ads, IMO pitches)
