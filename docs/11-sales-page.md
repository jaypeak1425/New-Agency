## Section 13: FAQ (Below the Fold)

**Q: Is this a software product?**
A: Yes. You log in, type or speak your scenario, and Atlas returns a full case design. You can also build pitch decks, send wholesaler handoffs, and track your pipeline in the dashboard.

**Q: Is the engine AI?**
A: Yes, but with important guardrails. The engine reasons from a locked, curated strategy library — it never invents. The recommendations are drawn from strategies Jay Peak and Luke Britt have used in real case-design meetings, plus industry-standard compliance review.

**Q: Does the engine replace my wholesaler?**
A: No. The engine builds the case design and writes the handoff email. Your wholesaler still provides the actual illustrations, the product knowledge, and the carrier relationships. The engine makes you a smarter customer of your wholesaler.

**Q: Does the engine replace my IMO/FMO/BGA?**
A: No. Most IMOs will offer this as a white-label to their agents. If your IMO doesn't, you can subscribe directly.

**Q: Is the engine compliant?**
A: Yes. Every output passes through a compliance filter. No "tax-free" language. No guarantee claims. No IRS form numbers in client copy. Outputs that don't pass are held for human review.

**Q: What if I don't find an opportunity in my first 30 days?**
A: We refund you. No questions asked. This isn't a free month — this is a money-back guarantee.

**Q: Can I cancel anytime?**
A: Yes. Monthly subscription, cancel anytime. Annual plans are available with a discount.

**Q: Who is Jay Peak?**
