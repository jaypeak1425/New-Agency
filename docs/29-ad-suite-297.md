## 5. The upsell call — subscriber → contracted producer (20 min)

**Trigger:** subscriber has run ≥3 scenarios or requested a handoff (they've felt the "aha").

1. **Open (pace):** "You've run [N] cases through Atlas. Which one surprised you?" *(let them
   sell themselves — consistency)*
2. **Deepen:** "What happens to that case now? Who places it, who's checking the design, who's
   in your corner at the carrier?" *(surfaces the isolation the software alone doesn't fix)*
3. **Frame:** "The software is how we think. The contract is us actually doing it WITH you.
   Atlas at no cost. Weekly one-on-one with Luke or Jay — on your live cases, not theory. A desk on your
   handoffs. Our paper, our desk, our override — which means we only make money when your
   cases place. Our economics are aligned with your growth. That's the whole model." `[alignment
   = trust; no cost = reciprocity]`
4. **Gate:** "It's not for everyone — there's a production floor and an interview, and the
   roster caps at 25. I'd rather tell you no now than waste your year." `[takeaway close;
   authority; real scarcity]`
5. **Close (choice, both forward):** "Want me to send the application, or do you want one more
   case review together first?" `[double bind — both answers advance]`

## 6. Contracting email sequence (subscribers, 5 emails / 3 weeks)

1. **"What the software can't do"** — honest limits: Atlas designs; it doesn't place, mentor,
   or negotiate underwriting. Introduce the desk. `[honesty = credibility + opens the gap]`
2. **"A case we placed"** — anonymized real story, numbers-lite, the mentor's role visible.
   `[social proof + future pace]`
3. **"The math of an override"** — transparent economics: "we take an override, which means we
   eat only when you do — compare that to a seminar invoice." `[alignment reframe]`
4. **"Why we cap at 25"** — the mentorship-scarcity rationale, straight. Application link.
   `[scarcity with a reason — reasons make scarcity believable]`
5. **"The door closes, the software stays"** — cohort deadline; the sub keeps everything
   either way. `[loss aversion without threat — status preserved]`

## 10. CPE seminar-in-a-box — the CPA/COI engine (docs/27 §7)

The Channel-3 asset kit. Version A is Luke & Jay's own; version B is the white-labeled module
contracted producers get in mentorship ("build your own COI referral network").

**Seminar (2 CE hours, live):** "What OBBBA Changed for Your High-Income Clients — and the
Planning Conversations They Expect You to Start."
1. **(20 min)** The 2026 landscape: permanent exemption at $15M, the AMT phase-out trap at
   $500K/$1M AMTI, what that does to $500K–$1.5M earners. *(CPAs get scored on exactly this.)*
2. **(30 min)** Three client fact patterns walked to a design: business-owner buy-sell gap ·
   qualified-money concentration at RMD age · illiquid estate above the exemption. Each ends
   with "here's the question to ask your client this quarter."
3. **(25 min)** The documentation lens: what a defensible file looks like per structure
   (the Case Atlas CPA-scrutiny checklists, presented as *their* professional armor).
4. **(15 min)** Working with an advanced-planning desk: what a clean referral looks like, what
   the CPA keeps (the client relationship, the tax work, the documentation role), what
   PeakBritt does (design, underwriting, placement). Q&A.

**Follow-up kit per attendee:** the Library's client-facing one-pagers for the three fact
patterns · the top-10-questions sheets (built to be a CPA's own due-diligence list — answering
their questions before they ask is the trust move) · a one-page "how referrals work with us."

**CPA outreach script (to book the seminar):**
> Subject: 2 CPE hours on the OBBBA changes — [City], [date]
> [Name] — we're hosting a 2-credit live session for CPAs on what OBBBA actually changed for
> high-income clients: the $15M exemption's planning windows and the AMT phase-out trap that
> catches $500K–$1.5M earners. No product talk — case patterns, documentation standards, and
> the questions your clients will be asking you this year. [Date/venue/registration]. Seats
> are limited to keep it discussion-sized.
> — [Front man / PeakBritt Financial Group]

**Producer module (mentorship deliverable):** the same deck + script templated so a contracted
producer can run it in their own market, with a playbook: sponsor CE properly or partner with
a provider · pick one niche (their book's dominant avatar) · one seminar/quarter · the
follow-up cadence that converts an attendee into a referring COI in ~90 days.
