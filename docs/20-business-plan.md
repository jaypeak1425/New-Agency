## 2. Path to $30,000 MRR

### Path 1 — Individual Agents Only
- $30,000 ÷ $97 = **~310 paying individual agents**
- Possible but slow

### Path 2 — IMOs at $75/seat/month (the leverage play)

| IMO Size | Seats | Monthly Revenue per IMO |
|---|---|---|
| Small IMO | 50 seats | $3,750/mo |
| Mid IMO | 100 seats | $7,500/mo |
| Large IMO | 500+ seats | $37,500+/mo |

**One large IMO at 500 seats = $37,500/mo = above your $30K target on its own.**

### Path 3 — The Realistic Path (months 1–12)

| Months | MRR Target | Composition |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 (build) | $0 | 2–3 design partners (1 mid IMO + 1 small IMO + 10–20 individual agents) free/beta |
| 4–6 (soft launch) | $3K–$6K | ~40 individual agents + 1 small IMO pilot converted to paid |
| 7–9 (public launch) | $10K–$15K | ~50 individual agents + 1 small IMO + 1 mid IMO (100 seats) |
| 10–12 (scale) | $25K–$35K | ~75–100 individual agents + 1 small IMO + 2 mid IMOs |

**By month 12, you're at $30K MRR with ~100 individual agents + 4 IMO relationships.** By month 18, if you land one large IMO (500+ seats), you're at $70K+ MRR.

## 3. Launch Plan (4 Phases)

### Phase 1 — Pre-Launch (Months 1–3): The Build
- Hire/contract the dev
- Run the 6-phase build plan
- Recruit 2 design partners (1 mid IMO + 1 small IMO + 10–20 individual agents)
- Design partners get free access in exchange for feedback
- Build the Recruiting Engine package
- Brand the agency, software, and bot names

### Phase 2 — Soft Launch (Months 4–6): Validation
- Open the doors to the first 50 paying individual agents
- Convert 1 design partner IMO to paid
- Launch the Recruiting Engine (organic + paid)
- Run the 5–7 email soap-opera sequence
- Show the product at 1 industry event
- Get 3 case studies from design partners

### Phase 3 — Public Launch (Months 7–9): IMO Outreach
- The Dream 100 IMO outreach begins
- First mid-size IMO contract signed
- Launch commercial produced and distributed
- The recruiting engine is running at full speed
- Hitting $10K–$15K MRR

### Phase 4 — Scale (Months 10–12): $30K MRR
- Second mid-size IMO contract signed
- The learning loop is producing real, defensible case-design data
- IMO pitch deck is refined from real pilot learnings
- Hitting $30K MRR

## 6. The Recruiting Engine — Marketing Stack

### For individual agents ($97/mo direct)
- One-pager: "The 5-min intro"
- Slide deck: 8–10 slides, demo-first pitch
- Demo video: 60–90s, the "you're in your car after an appointment" scenario
- Long-form sales page: the demo IS the page
- Email sequence: 5–7 emails, soap-opera style
- Organic social: 3–5 posts per week in Adventurer voice
- Paid ads: cold-audience versions of best-performing organic posts

### For IMOs ($75/seat white-label)
- IMO one-pager: "Add to your tech stack"
- IMO pitch deck: 15–20 slides, case-design brain story, retention angle
- IMO demo video: 3–5 min, white-label experience
- IMO sales page: long-form, in the language of an IMO principal
- IMO email sequence: separate from agent sequence
- Dream 100 outreach: 100 IMO principals
- LinkedIn outreach: IMO principals, senior recruiters
- Industry events: GAMA, NAIFA, MDRT, Advisorist

### For the discovery call (the demo that closes)
- Demo script: actual words, actual output
- "I've got a guy" walkthrough
- Customized per IMO: logo, brand, agents' actual book data

## 7. The Master Dashboard

### Per agent
- Plan, status, MRR contribution, signup date, channel
- Activity: prospects added, strategies recommended, pitches built, calls logged
- Engagement: logins/week, time in app, features used
- Pipeline: this week, this year, book-of-business opportunity, closed
- At-risk signals: declining activity, no logins in 14 days, unanswered tickets

### Per IMO
- Plan, seats purchased, seats active, seats churning
- MRR contribution, channel, signup date
- IMO-level reporting: which agents are getting value, which are at risk
- Compliance flags: outputs flagged for review

### For the team
- Total MRR, by segment, by plan
- Total customers, new this month, churned this month
- Channel attribution
- Activity trends
- **Opportunity Flow with Learning Loop** — every agent's recommendations, review on a call, verify/modify, engine learns
- **Compliance Flag Queue** — every output needing human review

## 8. The Pre-Launch Validation Process

**Step 1:** Strategy proposed (Jay, Luke, or senior producer says "add this strategy")
**Step 2:** Engine interviews the human team (asks the diagnostic questions)
**Step 3:** Engine requests documentation (illustrations, decks, marketing pieces)
**Step 4:** Human team provides documentation
**Step 5:** Engine drafts the strategy card in the uniform template
**Step 6:** Human team reviews and signs off
**Step 7:** Engine goes live with the strategy
**Step 8:** Learning loop kicks in — every recommendation is logged, every modification is captured, the next case is smarter

---

## 9. The Three Things That Decide Everything

1. **The IMO play is the leverage.** One large IMO at 500 seats = above $30K MRR on its own. Every IMO contract compounds.
2. **The pre-launch validation is the moat.** The brain gets smarter every time it's used. The longer the product runs, the harder it is to replicate.
3. **The brand is the wedge.** Elegant, premium, defensible — based on the agent's actual book, no false claims. The agent looks at their own numbers, not someone else's promise.
