## The Sequence Logic

The sequence follows the classic soap-opera emotional arc:

1. **Email 1:** The recruitment lie (acknowledge the pain)
2. **Email 2:** The invisible wall (show why the pain persists)
3. **Email 3:** The epiphany (introduce the new possibility)
4. **Email 4:** The "I've got a guy" moment (show the engine in action)
5. **Email 5:** The false belief destroyed (destroy the limiting belief)
6. **Email 6:** The soft ask (introduce the offer without pressure)
7. **Email 7:** The close (calendar link, plain, no hype)

**Send cadence:** Every 2-3 days. Total sequence length: ~18 days.

**Compliance check on every email:**
